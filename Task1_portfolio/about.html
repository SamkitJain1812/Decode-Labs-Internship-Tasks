A short note
about the person
behind these pages.

Hello.

I’m Samkit — a student and aspiring frontend developer currently completing the DecodeLabs Industrial Training Kit. I care about the small things: how a paragraph breaks, how a heading sits over a rule, how one column relates to the next.

My route into the web is a curious one. I started by editing HTML tables inside a school project template, slowly discovered the CSS box model, and then spent an embarrassing amount of time learning why margin collapses. Somewhere along the way, the puzzle of turning content into a clean, readable page became the thing I looked forward to at the end of every day.

I like designs that feel like they were written, not decorated. Editorial layouts, generous whitespace, and a serif that knows what it’s doing. My work leans toward black-and-white restraint because it forces every other decision — spacing, alignment, hierarchy — to actually earn its place.

Beyond code: I read too many essays, I take notes by hand, and I keep a running list of typefaces I’d like to use one day. If you have a project that values clarity over cleverness, I’d love to hear about it.
