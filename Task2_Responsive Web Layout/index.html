Our Story

Roasted with care, served with warmth.

Brew & Bean started in 2019 as a two-person espresso cart on a rainy Tuesday. Today we're a full café — but our promise hasn't changed: honest beans, patient technique, and a room that feels like a good friend's kitchen.

  • 01Beans sourced directly from farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala.
  • 02Small-batch roasted in-house every Sunday morning.
  • 03Pastries baked fresh daily by our friends at Rosemary Bakery next door.
What We Do

More than a cup of coffee.

Everything we serve is chosen for one reason — it makes the morning a little better.

Fresh Roasts

Single-origin beans roasted weekly in our back-room roastery. Never older than seven days.

Cozy Space

Soft chairs, warm light, free Wi-Fi, and outlets at every table. Stay as long as you like.

Quick Service

Order at the counter or skip the line with our app. Most drinks ready in under three minutes.

Fresh Pastries

Croissants, cardamom buns, and sourdough delivered every morning from the bakery next door.

Loyalty Perks

Every tenth drink is on us. Free birthday coffee. Little rewards, big smiles.

Events & Classes

Monthly cupping sessions, latte-art workshops, and open-mic Fridays. Everyone's welcome.
